2. Data on your device

Your game progress (progress, coins, records, unlocked content and settings) is stored only on your device. Depending on your settings, it is included in your device backup (on iPhone in iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer, on Android in your device’s Google backup). We do not receive this data. You can delete it at any time in the game under Settings → “Delete progress” or by deleting the app.

3. Game Center (Apple)

Using Game Center is optional. If you are signed in to Game Center in your device settings, the app sends your best distances (record and daily record) to Game Center so that the worldwide leaderboards work. To display them, the app loads the Top 100 of a leaderboard (Game Center names and scores) from Apple. Your Game Center name and your scores are visible to other players there.

Game Center is a service of Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland. Apple processes this data as an independent controller under the Apple Privacy Policy (see also Game Center & Privacy). We do not store this data. The legal basis for the transfer is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the leaderboards you choose to use). You can sign out at any time in your device settings.

4. App Store and statistics from Apple

The app is offered through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes data as an independent controller when you download or update the app. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple provides us with aggregated, anonymous usage figures and crash reports. They do not reveal who you are.

5. Google Play Games (Android)

In the Android version, using Google Play Games is optional. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app sends your best distances (record and daily record) to Google Play Games so that the worldwide leaderboards work, and loads the leaderboard (player names and scores) from Google to display it. Depending on your Google Play Games settings, your player name and scores are visible to other players there. To operate the service, Google also collects technical usage and diagnostic data as an independent controller.

Google Play Games is a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not store this data. The legal basis for the transfer is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the leaderboards you choose to use). You can manage and delete your profile and data in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.

6. Google Play and statistics from Google

The Android version is offered through Google Play. Google processes data as an independent controller when you download or update the app. If you have agreed to this on your device, Google provides us with aggregated install figures and anonymous crash and performance reports. They do not reveal who you are.

7. Contact by email

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We delete the message once it is no longer needed, unless we are legally required to keep it.

9. Children

The game is meant for all ages and does not collect any data from children either.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example in the country where you live. As the app does not collect personal data, these rights usually only concern emails you have sent us. Contact: support@mooslicht-studios.com.

11. Changes

If the app changes (for example with new features), we will update this policy. The current version is available in the app under Settings → Privacy policy and on this page.
